Human-AI Interaction · Labeling task

Which emotion is being expressed?

You'll read 5 short social media posts and choose the emotion each writer is expressing. It takes about 2–3 minutes.

  • Anonymous. We don't ask for your name, email or any personal details. We only save the labels you choose and how long each took, linked to a random ID.
  • Voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing this tab.
  • Content note. Some posts describe negative feelings such as sadness, anger or fear.

Instructions

How to label

  1. Read the post. It's shown all lowercase without punctuation because the dataset was cleaned that way.
  2. Pick the one emotion the writer is expressing, not how the post makes you feel.
  3. If more than one seems to fit, pick the strongest one. If none fits well, pick the closest. There are no right or wrong answers, so go with your first impression.
  4. Press Next to save your answer. You can't go back to earlier posts.
